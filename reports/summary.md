Generated by `python -m cres evaluate` from `reports/metrics.json`; do not edit by hand.

**Question.** Does what a consumer writes help predict a late company response, beyond knowing the company, product and issue?

**Answer, in numbers.**

- LR: adding the narrative to the structured fields changes test PR-AUC by -0.010 on average across 5 windows; the paired 95% CI is above zero in 0, below zero in 4 and spans zero in 1.
  Mean test PR-AUC: text 0.180, struct 0.614, both 0.605.
- LightGBM: adding the narrative to the structured fields changes test PR-AUC by -0.048 on average across 5 windows; the paired 95% CI is above zero in 3, below zero in 2 and spans zero in 0.
  Mean test PR-AUC: text 0.172, struct 0.509, both 0.460.

Chance PR-AUC (test prevalence) averages 0.020.

**Where the signal comes from.** Scoring complaints by the company's own trailing late rate alone, with no model, gives a mean test PR-AUC of 0.564. That column counts only the company's complaints received at least 30 days before the one being scored (checked by a unit test and by recomputing sampled rows from the live file). A few companies miss the deadline on most of their complaints, so high PR-AUCs here mostly reflect the company, not leakage.

## Read with care

- Windows W4, W5 test on complaints from the period when only a small share of complaints carried a published narrative, and that subset is late far more often; compare each window's PR-AUC with its own prevalence.
- Each window trains on the most recent year before its training cutoff.
- LR's C is chosen on validation average precision; LightGBM early-stops on validation average precision. Test is scored once.
- LightGBM's best validation round ranges from 3 to 1202 (median 62) out of up to 2,000: validation average precision peaks early and then falls, so these trees fit the training year more than the later quarter. Its parameters were not tuned.
- LR's chosen C sits at the edge of the searched grid (0.001 to 10) for: lr struct W4 (C=0.001), lr_balanced both W1 (C=0.001), lr_balanced struct W1 (C=0.001), lr_balanced struct W2 (C=0.001), lr_balanced struct W3 (C=0.001), lr_balanced struct W4 (C=0.001), lr_balanced struct W5 (C=0.001).
- LightGBM sees the 20,000 most frequent TF-IDF terms; LR sees the whole vocabulary.
- CIs: stratified bootstrap of the test split, 1,000 resamples; differences are paired on the same resamples.
